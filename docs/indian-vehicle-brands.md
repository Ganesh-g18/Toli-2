# Indian-market vehicle suggestions

Verified 14 October 2026. This is a curated identification list, not a claim of TOLI MOTORS service coverage or a complete brand registry. Only currently marketed passenger-vehicle brands from the requested starting list are suggested, with verified Tesla and McLaren additions. Legacy, privately imported and omitted brands can be typed manually.

The 35 current suggestions are: Aston Martin, Audi, Bentley, BMW, BYD, Citroën, Ferrari, Force Motors, Honda, Hyundai, Isuzu, Jeep, Kia, Lamborghini, Land Rover, Lexus, Lotus, Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, Maserati, McLaren, Mercedes-Benz, MG, MINI, Nissan, Porsche, Renault, Rolls-Royce, Škoda, Tata, Tesla, Toyota, VinFast, Volkswagen and Volvo. The rendered list uses locale-aware sorting.

Sources:
- [CarWale current new-car brand catalogue](https://www.carwale.com/new-cars/) covers all retained brands. Its generic brand index also includes Jaguar, so it is not sufficient evidence of an active lineup by itself.
- [Autocar India current car catalogue](https://www.autocarindia.com/cars) cross-checks mainstream/luxury/EV listings.
- [Tesla India configurator](https://www.tesla.com/en_in/modely/design) supports current Model Y sales.
- [VinFast India VF7](https://vinfastauto.in/en/vf7) and [official India launch](https://vinfastauto.com/vn_en/vinfast-makes-its-landmark-debut-with-launch-of-made-in-india-premium-electric-suvs-vf-6-and-vf-7) establish local offering.
- [Authorized McLaren India dealer](https://www.infinity-group.in/mclaren/gts-test-drive/) confirms Indian sales.
- [Lotus India lineup](https://www.autocarindia.com/cars/lotus) includes Emira, Emeya and Eletre.
- [Force Motors Gurkha launch](https://forcemotors.com/MEDIA_PAGE_PDF/Press_Release/2024/gurkhapr.pdf) confirms passenger SUV offering.

Excluded from the supplied starting list:
- **Abarth:** former India range discontinued; [CarDekho's April 2020 report](https://www.cardekho.com/india-car-news/bs6-effect-fiat-punto-range-and-linea-discontinued-25380.htm) and [Autocar discontinued Abarth Punto listing](https://www.autocarindia.com/cars/fiat/abarth-punto/variants).
- **Jaguar:** paused new-car offering during electric transition, not a claim that the brand permanently exited India. [Official India support page](https://www.jaguar.com/en-in/support.html) says new sales ended; [Autocar India September 2026 FAQ](https://www.autocarindia.com/cars/jaguar/) says no models are officially on sale. That page retains inconsistent legacy F-Pace introductory copy, so current FAQ and manufacturer's statement take precedence.

No upcoming-only brand was added based only on an announcement. Market availability can change; review this dated dataset when updating the form. Vehicle Model remains free text and optional; neither brand membership nor model membership is a validation requirement.
